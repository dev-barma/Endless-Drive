Endless Drive

Drive as far as you like through changing weather and time of day.

Use W and S to accelerate and brake, and A and D to steer. Open Help for all the controls.

Settings

Garage

Changes show on your vehicle right away.

Welcome

New here? Take a short tutorial to learn the controls, or skip it and start driving. You will only be asked once.

Help

Drive as far as you can, avoid traffic and enjoy the changing weather and time of day.

Accelerate: W or Up arrow
Brake: S or Down arrow (hold it when stopped to reverse)
Steer: A and D, or the Left and Right arrows
Horn: H
Nitro boost: hold Shift or N while accelerating
Headlights: L (auto, on or off)
Indicators: Q and E
Camera: C
Field of view: [ and ]
Car or motorbike: V
Time of day: T
Weather: Y
Pause: P or Esc
Restart: R

Endless mode never ends. In Regular mode, crashes damage your vehicle.
Green Hill Pass Tunnel, about 1 km long, appears along the route, and in Auto mode the headlights switch off inside and come back on at the exit if it is dark.
The Garage lets you change the vehicle, paint and rider gear, and there are three maps to choose from.
On a phone the touch controls appear automatically. The left and right pads steer, Gas, Brake and Nitro drive, and the small buttons handle the horn, lights, camera, indicators, time and weather. Hold Brake when stopped to reverse.
