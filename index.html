Help

Accelerate: W or Up arrow
Brake / reverse: S or Down arrow
Steer: A and D, or Left and Right arrows
Horn: H
Nitro: Shift or N
Gear up / down: X and Z
Headlights: L
Indicators: Q and E
Camera: C
Field of view: [ and ]
Car or bike: V
Time of day: T
Weather: Y
Pause: P or Esc
Restart: R
